\section{Introduction}
This document introduces the charging behaviour present in a resistor-capacitor (RC) circuit. When a capacitor $C$ is charged through a series resistor $R$ from a constant supply  $V_0$, the capacitor voltage rises according to
\begin{equation}
    V(t) = V_0 (1 - e^{-t/RC}),
\end{equation}
where the product RC has units of seconds and is called the time constant, $\tau = RC$
